    Factions

    The game's eight faction slots are fixed in its code: a mod can't add one, but it can give one of the four employers new names. The interface's faction text changes (PDA, contracts menu, trespassing and disguise messages, HQ labels); spoken lines and the HUD's flags stay.

    Sky & weather

    Terrain

    Click points along the ground; Enter finishes, Esc cancels. Saved roads become the game's road decals (terrain/<map>.decals.json); the view shows them after Save.

    Left-drag paints; middle-drag orbits, right-drag pans, scroll zooms; Esc leaves. Heights save as terrain/<map>.heights.changes.json (the build relights changed slopes), paint as terrain/<map>.layerNN.<texture>.changes.png. A layer paints over the ones listed before it. Objects keep their height: use Drop to ground on them.

    New squad

    Then click the ground: members stand in rows of up to 8 there, facing you (up to 40). A new encounter gets spawn point set A 10 m north.
