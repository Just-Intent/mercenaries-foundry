    Terrain

    Click points along the ground; Enter finishes, Esc cancels. Saved roads become the game's road decals (terrain/<map>.decals.json); the view shows them after Save.

    Left-drag paints; middle-drag orbits, right-drag pans, scroll zooms; Esc leaves.
